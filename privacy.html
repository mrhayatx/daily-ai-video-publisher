Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Daily AI Video Publisher processes information when an authorized user connects a TikTok account and publishes video content.

1. Information We Process

The application may process the following information:

  • Basic TikTok profile information provided through TikTok Login Kit.
  • TikTok account identifiers required for authorization.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens required to access TikTok APIs.
  • Videos, captions, hashtags and publishing preferences selected by the user.
  • Google Drive file names, identifiers and video files selected for publishing.
  • Technical logs, processing results and API error messages.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used only to:

  • Authenticate the authorized TikTok user.
  • Retrieve the account's available publishing options.
  • Upload and publish user-selected videos.
  • Monitor publishing status and identify processing errors.
  • Prevent unauthorized use and maintain application security.

3. User Authorization and Consent

TikTok access is granted only after the user completes TikTok's authorization process. The application does not request or store the user's TikTok password. The user controls the videos, captions, privacy settings and publishing instructions submitted through the application.

4. Data Storage and Security

Authorization credentials and workflow information may be stored within the application's secured automation environment. Reasonable technical measures are used to protect credentials and workflow data from unauthorized access, alteration or disclosure.

5. Data Sharing

Information is not sold or rented. Information is transmitted only to service providers required to operate the application, including TikTok, Google Drive and the automation hosting provider. These services process data according to their respective terms and privacy policies.

6. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the publishing workflow, maintain security and troubleshoot failed publishing attempts. Workflow records and credentials may be deleted when they are no longer required.

7. Revoking Access and Deletion

Users may revoke the application's TikTok access through their TikTok account settings. A user may also request deletion of application-related data and stored authorization credentials.

8. User Rights

Depending on applicable law, users may request access to, correction of or deletion of their personal information, or object to certain processing activities.

9. Children's Privacy

The application is not intended for children under the minimum age required to use TikTok. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application functionality, integrations or legal requirements change. The effective date shown above will be updated when material changes are made.

11. Contact

Privacy questions and data-deletion requests can be submitted through the application repository .
