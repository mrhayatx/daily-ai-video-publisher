Daily AI Video Publisher

A private automation tool for publishing AI-generated short videos from Google Drive to an authorized TikTok account.

About the Application

Daily AI Video Publisher helps its account owner prepare and publish short-form video content. TikTok access is provided only after the user explicitly authorizes the application.

The application does not request TikTok passwords and does not publish content to accounts that have not granted authorization.
