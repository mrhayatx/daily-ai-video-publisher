User Data Deletion Instructions

To request deletion of data associated with SocialAutomator, follow these steps:

  1. Email mrhayatx@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject: SocialAutomator Data Deletion Request.
  3. Include the name and email address associated with your Facebook or Instagram account.

After verification, associated account identifiers, access credentials and publishing records under our control will be deleted within 30 days. A confirmation will be sent after completion.

You may also remove the application's access through your Facebook account's Apps and Websites settings.
